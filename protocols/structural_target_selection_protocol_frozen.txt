STRUCTURAL TARGET SELECTION PROTOCOL

Goal:
Select additional independent self-correction targets without conditioning on
regeneration behavior, causal effects, favored outcomes, or model direction.

SOURCE POOL
Use the pre-existing qwen35 self-correction candidate pool generated before
the causal intervention results.

Exclude the eight trajectories already manually audited:
rows 164, 178, 106, 14, 79, 208, 29, 9.

ORDERING
Among remaining candidates satisfying the same pre-existing automated
candidate/position constraints, generate one fixed random ordering using seed
20260828.

Do not use the following during ordering or structural labeling:
- above_good / below_good direction
- final estimate
- favored/unfavored final side
- cot_covertness
- regeneration results
- causal intervention results

REVIEW PROCEDURE
Review candidates in the frozen random order in batches of 20.

During structural review show only:
- candidate correction thought
- local reasoning immediately before it
- local reasoning immediately after it

Mask:
- numerical values
- good/bad cause identity
- target/direction identity
- source row ID

STRUCTURAL LABELS

ACTIVE_DECISION:
The candidate is an explicit impartiality/self-correction thought occurring
while the numerical decision is still actively being reasoned about, AND
substantive numerical estimation/reconsideration occurs after the correction.

POST_COMMITMENT:
The correction occurs after the reasoning has effectively settled the answer
or as part of a final review/checklist, with no meaningful numerical decision
remaining.

PRE_NUMERICAL:
The correction occurs before substantive numerical estimation relevant to the
decision.

NOT_CORRECTION:
The candidate does not actually express the impartiality/self-correction
concept.

AMBIGUOUS:
The local context is insufficient to assign one of the above confidently.

CAUSAL ELIGIBILITY
Only ACTIVE_DECISION targets are causally eligible.

STOPPING RULE
Review the frozen ordering until either:
(a) 8 ACTIVE_DECISION targets have been obtained, or
(b) 60 candidates have been reviewed.

All eligible targets obtained under this rule will proceed to the regeneration
screen. Targets will not be removed based on regeneration rate or downstream
causal effect.

Regeneration rate determines whether a natural sentence-resampling comparison
is identifiable at that target; it does not determine whether the target is
reported.
