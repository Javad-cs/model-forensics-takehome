STRUCTURAL SELECTION IMPLEMENTATION ADDENDUM

The prospective pool is restricted to the same giraffes Donation Bet task.

Automated eligibility is defined without inspecting local context:
- question == giraffes
- primary_candidate == True
- 0.80 <= relative_position <= 0.95
- 800 <= remaining_chars <= 4500

Previously inspected rollouts are excluded regardless of whether they appeared
promising:
- Original eight audited rows:
  164, 178, 106, 14, 79, 208, 29, 9
- Additional previously manually inspected rows:
  60, 17, 102, 166

There must be at most one candidate event per rollout.
If a rollout contains multiple automatically eligible candidate events, select
the FIRST eligible event in reasoning order (smallest line_index).
This choice is made without reading candidate content.

The resulting rollout-level candidates are sorted by (row, line_index) and
then randomly permuted with Python random.Random(20260828).

Review proceeds in that frozen order.

Stopping rule:
Stop when 8 ACTIVE_DECISION targets have been obtained, after 60 reviewed
candidates, or when the eligible prospective pool is exhausted, whichever
comes first.

No target is selected or removed using:
- direction
- final estimate
- favored/unfavored side
- cot_covertness
- regeneration behavior
- downstream causal effect
